Today’s Cue · Wednesday, 30 September

Oil is back near $97, and FIIs just sold the most in three months

The Nifty slipped for a third straight session as crude rebounded and the rupee hovered near 96 to the dollar. Banks were the one bright spot.

Crude rebounded on fading hopes of a US–Iran resolution, and that set the tone for the day. Private banks held the index up while pharma and healthcare dragged it down.

Foreign investors were sellers for a fifth straight session, and domestic funds absorbed all of it. The market is holding steady for now, but it is leaning on local money to do so.
